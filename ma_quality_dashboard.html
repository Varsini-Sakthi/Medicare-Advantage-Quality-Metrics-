Methodology & data notes: This dashboard uses fully synthetic data generated to illustrate CMS Medicare Advantage Part C Star Ratings methodology — domain structure, measure weighting (1× standard, 3× for select outcome measures), and star cut-point banding. Quarterly figures represent simulated rolling 12-month interim rates, which plans commonly track between annual HEDIS submission cycles as leading indicators. Cut points are simplified/static for illustration and do not represent CMS's actual annually re-clustered thresholds. No real plan, member, or CMS data is used.
